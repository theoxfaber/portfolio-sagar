FAQ

Does Chatlode upload my chats?

No. There is no Chatlode server. Exports are files on your computer and the library lives in your browser's own storage. Read the privacy policy.

Which browsers are supported?

Microsoft Edge and Firefox now; Chrome is coming soon.

Will you support ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

Those already have many exporters. Next on our list are Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI and Mistral Le Chat.

It says "no messages found".

Reload the chat tab once after installing, wait for the answer to finish, then try again. If a site changed its layout, email us and we'll ship a fix.

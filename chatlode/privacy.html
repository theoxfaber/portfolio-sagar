Chatlode Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Chatlode browser extension for Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox.

Short version: Chatlode does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The chats you export or save stay in your own browser on your own device, and you can delete them at any time.

What Chatlode does

Chatlode reads the conversation that is open in your browser tab on perplexity.ai or chat.deepseek.com when you ask it to, and turns it into a file (Markdown, PDF, TXT or JSON) or saves it to a local library you can search, tag and organise.

Data the extension handles, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Text of the AI conversation you choose to export or save (your prompts, the answers, cited source links, the page URL and title)To create the export file or the library entry you asked forOnly in your browser: the file you download, and the extension's local storage (IndexedDB / storage.local). Never sent to us or any third party.
Tags, folder names, settingsTo organise your libraryOnly in your browser's extension storage
Your DeepSeek session (already in your browser)On DeepSeek, Chatlode asks DeepSeek's own website for the full message history of the chat you have open, using the login you already have, so long chats export completelyThe request goes only from your browser to chat.deepseek.com, the same site you are using. Chatlode does not store, copy or transmit your token anywhere else.

What Chatlode does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No reading of pages other than the supported chat sites, and nothing is read until you click an action.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data. We never receive your data in the first place.
  • No remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.

Permissions, explained

  • Access to perplexity.ai and chat.deepseek.com: to read the open conversation when you ask for an export.
  • storage: to keep your library, tags and settings on your device.
  • downloads: to save the export file to your Downloads folder.
  • activeTab: to know which tab you clicked the toolbar button on.
  • sidePanel (Chrome and Edge) / sidebar (Firefox): to show your library next to the page.

Paid "Pro" licences (future)

Pro is not on sale yet and this version makes no licensing network requests. If Pro launches, activating a licence will send only the licence key you type and a random device label to the payment provider named on the purchase page (for example Lemon Squeezy or Dodo Payments) to confirm the key is valid. Your chats are never sent. This policy will be updated, with a new effective date, before that happens.

Deleting your data

Delete any chat from the library with one click, or remove all Chatlode data by uninstalling the extension, which deletes its local storage. Files you downloaded are ordinary files on your computer that you control.

Children

Chatlode is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
